FROM STREETS
TO STARS

Originally a stray named Kudryavka (Little Curly), she was found wandering the freezing streets of Moscow. She was chosen not for her breed, but for her calmness under pressure.

The Wikipedia Record: On Nov 3, 1957, Laika became the first living creature to orbit Earth. One of the scientists later said: "Laika was quiet and charming... I wanted to do something nice for her because she had so little time left to live."

She was the pioneer for all of humanity. While history says she stayed in orbit, the $LAIKA community knows she was the first soul to reach the Moon. We are her legacy.
